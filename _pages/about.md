I work on EEG, machine learning and apps. Here are some of my projects:

- **[Cerebro](https://github.com/metal-carp2/Cerebro)**: an app that replays EEG recordings and scores them with a machine learning model.
- **[EEG Research](https://github.com/metal-carp2/EEG_Reaserch)**: EEGNet baselines for decoding imagined movement from brain signals.
- **[Speech Emotion Sessions](https://github.com/metal-carp2/Sentiment-Analysis-Model)**: a terminal app that listens to a short recording and maps emotion over time.
- **[Metamaterial Diffusion](https://github.com/metal-carp2/metamaterial-diffusion)**: simulates acoustic metamaterials whose hollow cavities resonate to block sound.
- **[Recycling App](https://github.com/metal-carp2/RecyclingAppFinal)**: a mobile app for scanning recyclables, finding drop-off centers and estimating payouts.
