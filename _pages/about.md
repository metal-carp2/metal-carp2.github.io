Hi, I'm Adithya. I like building things that make sense of signals, especially the ones that come from the brain.

Most of my time goes into EEG and machine learning. I've trained EEGNet models to decode imagined movement from brain activity, and I'm building Cerebro, an app that replays EEG recordings and scores them with a model right on the device. I'm also curious about other kinds of signals: I've built a tool that tracks emotion in speech, and a simulator for acoustic metamaterials that block sound using resonating cavities.

Outside of research I enjoy making apps that people can actually use, like a recycling app that helps you find drop-off centers and estimate what your recyclables are worth.

Projects
======

- **[Cerebro](https://github.com/metal-carp2/Cerebro)**: an app that replays EEG recordings and scores them with a machine learning model.
- **[EEG Research](https://github.com/metal-carp2/EEG_Reaserch)**: EEGNet baselines for decoding imagined movement from brain signals.
- **[Speech Emotion Sessions](https://github.com/metal-carp2/Sentiment-Analysis-Model)**: a terminal app that listens to a short recording and maps emotion over time.
- **[Metamaterial Diffusion](https://github.com/metal-carp2/metamaterial-diffusion)**: simulates acoustic metamaterials whose hollow cavities resonate to block sound.
- **[Recycling App](https://github.com/metal-carp2/RecyclingAppFinal)**: a mobile app for scanning recyclables, finding drop-off centers and estimating payouts.
